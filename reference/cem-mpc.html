CEM-MPC

The planner you will keep when the model stops being env.step. Vanilla only: no colored noise, no elite memory.

Loop

copy model state
for each of N action sequences of length H:
    restore, step H times, cost = sum of step costs
keep K lowest-cost sequences (elites)
mean, std ← elite statistics
execute best_sequence[0]
mean ← shift left, repeat last action
replan from the new real observation

Do not

  • Call reset to evaluate a candidate (new random pose / goal).
  • Plan through the TimeLimit wrapper on Pendulum (imagination spends the real budget).
  • Execute the unused Gaussian mean; execute a sequence you scored.
  • Rewind the car but not sim.steps (actions apply only when steps % 3 == 0).
