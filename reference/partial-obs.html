Cheat sheet

Partial observations

Why vx, vy are state, how to hide them, and how to score the damage.

Vocabulary

TermIn this repo
StateBicycle: pose + speed. Complete enough that s′ = f(s, a).
ObservationWhat f is allowed to see. May omit speed.
Fully observedDefault six-vector x, y, vx, vy, cos_h, sin_h.
Partially observedPose-only x, y, cos_h, sin_h. Same pose, many speeds.

Source: Spinning Up, States and Observations. The name POMDP: Kaelbling, Littman, Cassandra 1998.

Bicycle (why speed is not optional)

beta = arctan(0.5 * tan(steering))
v    = speed * [cos(heading+beta), sin(heading+beta)]
position += v * dt
heading  += speed * sin(beta) / (LENGTH/2) * dt
speed    += acceleration * dt

dt is 1/15 s inside physics, 0.2 s per action at 5 Hz. Stored x, y are meters/100. Stored vx, vy are (m/s)/5.

Masks

NameInput to fPredicts
fullsix-vector + actionnext six-vector
posex, y, cos_h, sin_h + actionnext pose
stacktwo poses + actionnext pose

Stack is meant to recover Δpose / 0.2 s. In stored units the delta is tiny, so the curve often hugs pose-only. That is still a hand-built history, not a learned z.

Fair score

pose_mse = mean((x, y, cos_h, sin_h) error²)
open-loop: ŝ ← f(ŝ, a) for H = 1, 2, 5, 10, 20

Do not compare overall MSE across masks. Dropping vx, vy removes the largest identity terms. Full one-step pose MSE must beat identity. Pose-only should be worse by H=10.

Do not

  • Hold out the only CEM episode (too few). Keep CEM in train; score curves on random episodes.
  • Call a lower pose-only training loss a win.
  • Replace the stack with a GRU until the stack plot exists.
  • Start pixels. Missing velocity is the kinematic version of missing the rest of the world.

Sources: Spinning Up, Vehicle.step, Hausknecht & Stone, DRQN.
